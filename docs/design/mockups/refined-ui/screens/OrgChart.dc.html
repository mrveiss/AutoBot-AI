Like a film script: people and agents are the actors, a role is the part they play. A role also holds skills: processes need them to run, and a meta skill is a reusable set of processes that describes how to do a task. Every process has an owner role, can be reused by several workflows, and can run on its own on a routine. Recast a role and all of its work moves with it. Open process canvas
