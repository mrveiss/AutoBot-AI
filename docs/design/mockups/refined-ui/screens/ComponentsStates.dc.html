Elevation

Use for: deciding how layers separate.
--bg · page
--surface + 1px --border · card, nav, menu, dialog
--surface2 + --line · hover, table head, inset, current item
--surface again · input inside an inset

No shadows. Separation comes from 1px borders (2px in High contrast) and surface steps: bg → surface → surface2. Overlays (menus, dialogs, sheets) sit on a scrim and keep their border; nothing uses box-shadow, text-shadow, drop-shadow or gradients.

Rules: at most three surface steps in one view. If two neighbours share a surface, a border separates them; never both a border and a fill change on the same edge.
