HostSo you moved the whole team off hosted APIs last spring. What was the actual trigger — cost, or something else?
GuestHonestly, latency first, cost second. Our support agents were waiting two or three seconds per call, and once we put a 14B model on a local box with a decent GPU, that dropped under four hundred milliseconds.
HostAnd quality held up?
GuestFor retrieval-heavy work, yes. The model matters less than the knowledge base behind it. Once chunking and re-ranking were right, the smaller model was answering from our own docs instead of guessing.
GuestThe part people underestimate is operations. You now own model updates, GPU drivers, backups of the vector store. We ended up treating the inference nodes like any other fleet — Ansible, health checks, a runbook for restarts.
Speaker 3Did you try running anything on NPUs, or is it all GPU?
GuestWe benchmarked OpenVINO on a couple of NPU laptops for embeddings. Good enough for background indexing, not for chat. edited
HostLet's dig into that after the break — I want to hear how you split workloads between the two.
